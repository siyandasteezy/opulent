About Us

A Legacy of
Exceptional Design

Established in 2013, Opulent Interior D-Zines is a Proudly South African company 100% owned and managed by a young, black female professional. We maintain a Level 1 BBBEE status and are proudly headquartered at the Riversands Incubation Hub in Midrand, Gauteng.

Our team consists of qualified interior designers and decorators, artisans, carpenters, stitchers, framers, project managers, hospitality consultants, architects, structural engineers, landscaping engineers, property managers and best selected service providers — all united by a passion for aesthetics, luxury, quality and durability.
